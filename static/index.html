A little more about Pace.

Pace is an experimental learning companion by Swayam Bangad · Pace Lab, built for Neural Storm 2026, HI-03.

How learning adapts

A Python Bayesian knowledge-tracing model updates a practice estimate from your answers. It recommends smaller steps after an incorrect answer and further practice or moving on after correct answers. Its parameters are illustrative and have not been calibrated on students. It never uses reading speed or infers a disability.

Where the content comes from

Sample lessons are original, prepared teaching passages. Your pasted lessons use local TF-IDF sentence ranking and word-recall questions. Optional cloud AI is used only when configured and explicitly selected. These modes are labelled in the reader.

Your data stays under your control

When remembering progress is enabled, lesson text, answers and preferences are saved in this browser. Lesson processing happens on the app server, which doesn't keep a lesson database. Cloud mode sends lesson text to its configured provider. Browser narration may use device or browser voice services. No diagnosis, camera or microphone is needed.

Built with accessibility in mind

Keyboard navigation, adjustable lesson text, optional narration and clear feedback are part of the design. Accessibility and educational effectiveness need ongoing testing with intended users. A quiz estimate is not a diagnosis or proof of mastery.

Clear this browser's data?

This removes your saved lessons, preferences and practice history from this browser. You can export your progress first.
